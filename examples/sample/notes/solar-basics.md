# Solar Basics

## Photovoltaic panels

Rooftop solar uses photovoltaic panels made of silicon cells. Each solar panel produces direct current,
and the inverter converts it to alternating current for the house. Panel orientation and roof shading
decide most of the annual yield. A south-facing roof with little shading is ideal for solar panels.

## Inverter and net metering

The inverter also reports production data. Under net metering the utility company credits surplus
solar energy exported to the grid. Pairing solar panels with a home battery keeps more of that energy
on site; see [battery storage](battery-storage.md).

## Sizing

Size the solar array from annual electricity consumption. A heat pump raises electricity consumption,
so size the solar array after deciding on the heat pump.
