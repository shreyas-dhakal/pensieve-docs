# Battery Storage

## Home battery

A lithium-ion battery stores surplus solar energy for the evening peak. A home battery also gives
backup power during a grid outage if the inverter supports islanding. Battery capacity is measured
in kilowatt-hours; usable capacity is lower than the nameplate capacity.

## Economics

The payback period of a home battery depends on the gap between the retail tariff and the
net metering export rate. When the utility company pays little for exported solar energy,
battery storage pays back faster. See [[solar-basics]].

## Lithium-ion vs lithium ion phosphate

Lithium ion phosphate cells trade energy density for cycle life and safety.
