Heat Pumps
==========

An air-source heat pump moves heat from outside air into the house. Its efficiency is measured as the
coefficient of performance. Insulation and draught proofing come first: a leaky house needs a larger
heat pump and raises electricity consumption.

Running costs
-------------

A heat pump runs on electricity, so cheap solar energy from rooftop solar panels lowers running costs
in spring and autumn. In winter the heat pump draws grid electricity at the retail tariff.
